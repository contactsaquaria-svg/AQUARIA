📅 Return Window

We accept returns and exchange requests within 7 days from the date of delivery.

To initiate a return or exchange, email concierge@aquariabrand.com or WhatsApp us with your Order ID and reason. Our team will respond within 24 hours to arrange pickup.

✅ Eligibility Conditions

To be eligible for a return or exchange, items must meet the following conditions:

  • Unworn, unused and in original condition
  • Original tags attached and intact
  • Original dust bag and presentation box included
  • No signs of wear, perfume, staining or damage
  • Footwear tried on only on soft carpeted surfaces (never outdoors)

❌ Non-Returnable Items

  • Items purchased during Final Sale or clearance events
  • Customised, monogrammed or personalised products
  • Items showing clear signs of use, damage or missing original packaging

Privacy & Data Protection Policy

Strict Confidentiality & 256-Bit Encrypted Security

🔒
Your privacy is our highest priority.
AQUARIA operates with complete transparency. We collect only the minimum data necessary and never sell or trade your personal information under any circumstances.

📋 Information We Collect

When you shop with AQUARIA, we collect the following information solely to process and deliver your order:

  • Full name and delivery address
  • Mobile number and email address (for order updates and tracking)
  • Payment confirmation reference (we do not store card numbers or CVV)
  • Order history and product preferences (to improve your shopping experience)
  • Device and browser data via cookies (for website performance only)

✅ How We Use Your Information

  • Processing and fulfilling your orders
  • Dispatching tracking information and delivery updates
  • Sending order confirmations and customer service communications
  • Sending AQUARIA newsletters (only if you opt-in; unsubscribe anytime)
  • Improving website experience and product recommendations

🔐 Data Security & Encryption

All transactions on the AQUARIA website are processed through RBI-approved payment gateways (Razorpay / Cashfree) using 256-bit SSL encryption.

We never store your credit card, debit card numbers, CVV or UPI PIN on our servers at any time.

Our website is secured with industry-standard HTTPS protocols and regular security audits.

🚫 Zero Third-Party Data Sharing

AQUARIA will never sell, rent, exchange or share your personal information with any third-party marketing company, data broker or advertising network.

Your data is shared only with our trusted logistics partners (Blue Dart / Delhivery) solely for the purpose of delivering your order.

🤝 Your Rights

You have full rights over your personal data stored with AQUARIA:

  • Access: Request a copy of all data we hold about you
  • Correction: Update any inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request complete erasure of your account data
  • Unsubscribe: Opt out of marketing communications at any time

To exercise any of these rights, email us at concierge@aquariabrand.com and we will respond within 48 business hours.
